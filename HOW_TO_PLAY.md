# How to Play — Starfall: Zero Hour

## Your mission

Clear each space arena, collect credits, and upgrade your blaster and shield. A Warden boss appears every 10 sectors.

## Controls

- **W, A, S, D** — Move with smooth acceleration
- **Mouse right / left** — Turn right / left
- **Mouse up / down** — Look up / down
- **Left mouse button** — Fire
- **R** — Reload plasma cells
- **Shift** — Sprint faster
- **Esc** — Pause or resume
- **F3** — Show or hide the frame-rate display

Click Launch to start. The game captures the mouse while you play. Press Esc to pause and release the mouse.

## Staying safe

Watch your hull bar at the lower left. Red enemy bolts can hurt your ship, so keep moving and dodge sideways. If your hull reaches zero, choose Retry sector to try again.

Your blaster holds 24 plasma cells. Press R to reload. It also reloads automatically when empty.

## Credits and upgrades

Defeat a drone to earn credits. Clearing a sector gives a bonus. Bosses award extra credits.

Between sectors, spend credits on:

- **Heavy bolts** — Deal more damage
- **Rapid coil** — Fire more quickly
- **Shield plating** — Increase maximum hull

Prices rise with each upgrade level. Credits and upgrades save automatically in this browser on this device.

## Warden boss

A Warden appears at sectors 10, 20, 30, and every tenth sector after that. Watch its health bar at the top. It fires a spread of bolts, so moving sideways helps you avoid them.

## Difficulty and settings

Choose Cadet, Pilot, or Ace on the launch screen. Open Settings from the pause screen or hangar to change mouse sensitivity, sound, and graphics quality.
