# Starfall: Zero Hour

A little 3D space shooter you play in a web browser. Fight AI drones, earn credits, upgrade your blaster, and take on a boss every 10 sectors.

## Download and play

1. Click the green **Code** button above, then choose **Download ZIP**.
2. Unzip the downloaded folder.
3. Double-click **launch-game.bat** to open the game in your browser. You can also open **index.html** directly in Edge, Chrome, or Firefox.
4. Click **Launch**, pick a difficulty, and play.

No install or internet connection is needed after downloading. This version runs in your browser; it does not include a separate EXE.

## How to play

- **W, A, S, D** — Move
- **Mouse** — Aim
- **Left click** — Shoot
- **R** — Reload
- **Shift** — Sprint
- **Esc** — Pause
- **F3** — Show the FPS

Defeat enemies to earn credits. Between sectors, spend credits on blaster and shield upgrades. Your progress is saved in your browser on your computer.

More player instructions are in [HOW_TO_PLAY.md](HOW_TO_PLAY.md).

## Features

- 3D space arena
- AI drone opponents
- Warden boss every 10 sectors
- Credits and ship upgrades
- Three difficulty settings
- Mouse sensitivity, sound, and graphics settings
- Local progress saving
- Offline play

## Made with

- HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- WebGL for the 3D graphics

There are no external libraries or game-engine downloads. The game aims for 60 FPS and adjusts visual quality if it slows down; actual performance depends on your computer.

For notes about adding online multiplayer later, see [MULTIPLAYER_NOTES.md](MULTIPLAYER_NOTES.md).
